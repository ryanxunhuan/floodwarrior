Decision Support & Visualization

This team designs and evaluates visual tools for understanding where floods may occur and how conditions change over time.

Led by Nikola Banovic

Visual tools for flood decisions

The team works with the Anthropology team to identify users’ information needs and study how planners explore flood-model outputs. These findings guide the design of maps and time controls for decisions that unfold over time.

The team evaluates successive prototypes that combine geographic views with forecast information. Its studies examine how people compare plausible futures, interpret uncertainty, and move between spatial and temporal views.

Research Focus

  • Identify workflows, information needs, and barriers through design with users.
  • Prototype visualizations of flooding, forecast changes, and uncertainty.
  • Evaluate how visualization choices affect comprehension and task performance.

Connections Across the Project

Physical forecasts and uncertainty estimates inform the visual tools, while human and institutional research shapes their design. User feedback guides the next revisions.
