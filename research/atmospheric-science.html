Atmospheric Science

High-resolution rainfall ensembles for flood forecasting.

Led by Francina Dominguez

Rainfall Forecast Ensembles

The Atmospheric Science team uses the Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF) model to simulate heavy rainfall at high resolution. These simulations capture small-scale convective motions that can produce intense, flood-causing rainfall.

By varying initial conditions and model physics, the team builds an ensemble: a set of simulations representing plausible outcomes for the same storm. Differences among these simulations describe uncertainty in rainfall timing, location, and amount, helping inform decisions before an event.

Rainfall at Detroit Arsenal

The members vary in the timing of the heaviest rain and in the final accumulation. These rainfall trajectories can serve as inputs to a hydrology model, carrying precipitation uncertainty into flood forecasts for Detroit Arsenal.

Rainfall Percentiles

At each location, the ensemble’s rainfall totals define a distribution. The 50th percentile is its median, while the 75th and 90th percentiles show progressively wetter outcomes. The 90th percentile can help planners examine a high-rainfall scenario.
